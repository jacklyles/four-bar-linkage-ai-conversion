A planar four-bar linkage has four joints, so its configuration is described by four numbers. Because the linkage lengths are constrained, the reachable configurations form a one-dimensional configuration space. The plot at left projects that space onto the two joint angles θ and φ; the drawing at right shows the corresponding linkage.
